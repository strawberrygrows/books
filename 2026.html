Books I read in 2026

The Iliad, by Homer
I read this aloud in a book club with some friends, over the course of 18 months. It was magnificent. We had two other translations for reference, but didn't read them cover-to-cover: in English, nothing can compete with iambic pentameter.
The Invisible Life of Addie LaRue, by V. E. Schwab
Faustian bargain with romantic characteristics. Great book to read on the plane.
Anna Karenina, by Leo Tolstoy
I found the structure of this book somewhat frustrating, but I can't deny it's brilliant. I'm particularly fond of Levin.
