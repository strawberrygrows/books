Introduction
Understanding Your Recovery

Your operation may have been performed through your abdomen, your vagina, or via laparoscopy (keyhole surgery). Recovery varies depending on your age, lifestyle, and fitness before the operation.

Important: Although your outside scar may heal quickly, it takes longer to heal on the inside. Give yourself time to fully recover.

Circulatory Exercises

Do these every few hours until you're back to full mobility

  • Sitting upright, take a deep breath in \u2014 feel your lower ribs move outwards. Repeat 4\u20135 times every hour and every time you wake
  • Move your feet and ankles up and down for 30 seconds
